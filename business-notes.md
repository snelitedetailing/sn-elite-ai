## Pricing

- Starting prices need a **current, approved source** before they are quoted,
  posted or used in any calculation. No approved price list is stored in this
  repository. If none is supplied, say pricing is unconfirmed and send the
  customer to the quote page instead of naming a number.
- Leads & Bookings notes record amounts already sent to some leads (e.g.
  $159, $200, $220) and past job prices. These are **history, not an approved
  price list**. Don't repeat them in new drafts unless the owner confirms them.

## Service promises

- **Stain removal is not guaranteed.** Never promise it; describe it as an
  attempt whose result depends on the stain.
